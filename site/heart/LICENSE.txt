Heart model in this folder (heart.glb, heart-graph.json): CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Derived from:
- Z-Anatomy, the libre 3D atlas of anatomy (Gauthier Kervyn et al.), CC BY-SA 4.0
  https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
- which derives from BodyParts3D, The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/

Changes: converted to RAS millimetres, compressed (meshopt); heart-graph.json adds vertex positions,
areas and hand-placed conduction landmarks for the electrophysiology simulation.
Only the heart chambers, papillary muscles and valve leaflets are used.
